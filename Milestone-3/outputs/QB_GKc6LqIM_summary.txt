color correction really is a process of experimentation trying all the sliders to the left to the right until you find The Sweet Spot on each so I'm going to leave my contrast around here. The next setting that is directly related to contrast is Pivot which essentially has to do with which parts of your image will get these contrast adjustments added. If you are a beginner I'd really advise keeping your color correction workflow as simple as possible especially in the early days. All I needed really was just the basics of exposure and color which meant over time I was able to speed up my workflow exponentially and make way more content.
